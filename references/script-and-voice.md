# Script & voiceover

## Writing the script for the client to record

- Length: speech runs ~2.1–2.6 words/s in most Latin-script languages at an advert pace (measure the client's first take) → **120–140 words ≈ 55–60 s**.
- One idea per sentence; each sentence becomes one scene (8–12 sentences). Short sentences record cleanly.
- Arc that worked: aspiration or pain hook (what the product/service changes for the viewer) → why it matters (3 concrete benefits) →
  a one-line pivot question ("Et si vous commenciez dès aujourd'hui ?") → "Bienvenue à <name>, <what/where>" →
  levels / offer → formats (on site, intensive, online) → reassurance (accommodation, support) → future/proof →
  "Rejoignez <name>" + 3 outcome adjectives → brand line + call to action.
- Say the brand name 2–3 times (intro, join, sign-off). Give the URL as people say it ("brandname dot com").
- Facts only from the client (years, number of graduates, programmes). No prices unless asked.
- Deliver it as plain text with the timing per line and these recording tips:
  quiet soft-furnished room, AC/fans off, phone ~20 cm away slightly off-axis, one take with ~1 s silence between
  sentences, repeat a whole sentence after a stumble, 55–65 s total, send the original WAV/M4A/MP3.
- AI voices are fine too (e.g. an ElevenLabs narrator voice in the client's language, generated by the client). The free local Kokoro voice
  was rejected by the client — only offer it as a last resort.

## When the recording arrives

- The client may change the wording. Their recording + their text are now the truth: rewrite `SCRIPT.md` with their
  sentences, one 4-space-indented line each (that's what the scripts parse), and rebuild the storyboard around them.
- `split_voice.py` cuts at pauses inside the right gaps; if it reports too few pauses, lower `--min-pause` or raise
  `--noise`. If a line's words-per-second is far off the others, a boundary jumped — nudge with `--min-pause` or
  hand-edit `voice_split.json` and rerun the cut with ffmpeg.
- `align_words.py` transcribes each clip separately (reliable) and aligns to the script text; ≥ 90 % matched words is
  normal; estimated words sit between neighbours. Never transcribe the whole file in one pass with an initial prompt
  — on a real project that skipped the first 19 s and looped a phrase.
- Known environment issues: Hugging Face's default downloader can stall for hours on slow links — the script uses
  plain HTTP downloads; PyAV inside faster-whisper may crash with `metadata_errors` — the script decodes audio with
  ffmpeg instead.

## Captions

Captions come from `audio_meta.json` word times (frame-relative) via `captions.mjs` and the preset caption skin;
they are worth keeping for social (most feeds autoplay muted). They show the client's exact words.
