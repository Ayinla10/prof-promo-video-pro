# Discovery — questions and suggestions before any work

A promo built on wrong assumptions costs hours of rendering to redo. So before installing, capturing or writing
anything, run this interview. Use the AskUserQuestion tool (max 4 questions per call, 2–4 options each, put the
recommended option FIRST with "(Recommended)", always let "Other" carry free text), in short rounds. Skip any
question the user already answered in their message or that earlier work in the conversation settles — re-asking
known facts is annoying. Summarise what you already know before each round so the user can correct it.

Each option's description should carry a **suggestion** (why it's a good choice, what it costs, what it changes),
not just restate the label. Users of this skill are business owners and freelancers, not engineers — plain words.

## Round 1 — the client and the goal (always)

1. **Client & website** — "Which client is this for, and what's their website?" (free text via Other if needed;
   offer "No website — I'll send material" as an option).
2. **Goal of the ad** — More enquiries / sign-ups / bookings (Recommended for most local businesses) · Brand awareness · A specific
   event, intake or season · Launch of a product/service · Recruitment. *Suggestion:* the goal decides the hook and the call to action.
3. **Audience** — who must say "that's for me": e.g. young adults · parents · professionals · businesses (B2B) · tourists ·
   a diaspora or a foreign-language community. Ask for the country/region if they run paid ads.
4. **Language** — voiceover + captions language(s); offer "both versions" (doubles the voice work, not the design).

## Round 2 — format and content rules

1. **Formats** — Both 16:9 + 9:16 (Recommended) · 9:16 only (TikTok/Reels/Status) · 16:9 only · also a 1:1 feed
   version. *Suggestion:* the vertical version is where most views come from on phones.
2. **Length** — 60 s (Recommended: full story) · 30 s · 15 s teaser · "60 s + 15 s cut-down" (Suggest this for paid
   ads: the 15 s reuses the hook + CTA scenes, little extra work).
3. **Prices & offers** — No prices (Recommended: ad stays valid longer, drives enquiries) · show a "from" price ·
   a promo/discount; and **date** — timeless "now open / book now" (Recommended) · a specific date (event, intake, sale).
4. **Call to action** — WhatsApp (Recommended where WhatsApp is the main channel) · phone · website · visit the
   address · several. Confirm the exact number/URL later from `contacts.md`.

## Round 3 — material

1. **Voiceover** — Client records it from a script I write (Recommended: real voice, best trust) · client already
   has a recording · AI voice they generate (ElevenLabs etc.) from my script · free local voice (warn: robotic).
2. **Footage** — "Send links (Facebook/YouTube/Drive/TikTok) of your videos" · photos only · none. *Suggestion:*
   even 3–4 seconds of real class/shop/team footage makes the ad feel real; phone clips are fine.
3. **Music** — "I have royalty-free tracks" · "Pick for me" (suggest free sources with clear licences: YouTube Audio
   Library, Pixabay Music, freetouse.com — note credit lines) · no music.
4. **Proof to feature** — testimonials (names/quotes/video), numbers (years, graduates, clients), awards,
   accreditations, partner logos. Only real ones.

5. **Reference videos** — "Send 1–3 videos you love (TikTok/Reels/YouTube links)". *Suggestion:* the fastest way
   to match their taste; study them with `reference-analysis.md` before concepts.
6. **Sound** — music + synced sound effects (Recommended: what makes motion graphics feel produced) · sound effects
   only · music only. Music from a link they choose is fine to download once they've picked it.

## Round 4 — style (ask after capturing the website, when you can show the brand)

1. **Look** — stay close to the website's brand (Recommended) · bolder/more energetic · premium/minimal; and
   **light or dark mode** (a light warm-white canvas with brand-colour glows reads fresh on social).
   **Hero** — a character (e.g. a mascot built from the brand mark: an orb with eyes) · a hero object (a line, a card)
   · footage-led. *Suggestion:* a character is the most memorable and shareable.
2. **Mood & pace** — energetic & joyful · warm & reassuring · prestigious & calm.
3. **Must include / must avoid** — specific photos, people who must (not) appear, words to avoid, competitors.
4. **Where it will run** — organic posts · paid ads (then safe zones for platform buttons matter more, and a 15 s
   cut-down is worth it) · website hero · TV/event screens (then no captions needed, higher bitrate).

## Suggestions to make proactively

After the website capture, before the storyboard, propose **2–3 creative concepts** in one message and recommend
one — each with: the hook line, the signature visual motif, the arc, and which client assets it leans on. Examples of
motif ideas: see `art-direction.md` → §1 (motif table by sector).

Also suggest, when relevant:
- a **script draft** written to the chosen concept (~120–140 words for 60 s), clearly marked as editable;
- the **best photos/clips** found on the site/footage for each scene (with reasons), and gaps worth filling
  ("a 5-second clip of a class in session would make scene 3 much stronger");
- **posting tips**: music credit line, caption text with the CTA, first-frame hook for the 9:16, a 15 s cut-down;
- **risks**: stale contacts on the site, footage with other people's music, photos of minors, burned-in subtitles.

## When to stop asking

Stop once you know: client + site, goal, audience, language, formats + length, prices/date rule, CTA, voice plan,
footage/music plan. Anything else can be decided with a sensible default — state the default instead of asking.
Never ask more than ~4 rounds; respect a user who says "just go" (use the Recommended defaults and list them).
