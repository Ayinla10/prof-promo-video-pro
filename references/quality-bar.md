# Quality bar — the beauty pass before any render

Score every scene from the snapshot contact sheet (2–3 stills per scene). A scene ships only if **every line is
"yes"** and its overall score is ≥ 8/10. Anything less is redesigned (new layout or treatment), not nudged —
small nudges rarely lift a weak frame. Re-snapshot after fixing.

## Motion checks (scan the 1–1.5 s contact sheets of every act first)

- **Never a slide:** in every still something is mid-motion (camera drift, blob drift, a floating card, the hero
  blinking); no element sits frozen for more than ~1 s.
- **Continuity:** each act is one camera world; stations are reached by camera moves, not cuts; the end of each act
  hands off into the next (dive / burst / launch / whip).
- **The hero acts:** the character/object does something on each sentence (reacts, hops, carries, transforms).
- **Transformations:** at least 2–3 per act where an element turns into the next one instead of being replaced.
- **Clean entrances/exits:** nothing peeks before its entrance (off-screen starts, masked pills, blurred glyphs);
  nothing lingers after its exit.
- **Safe areas:** nothing in the caption band; portrait content between y 200 and ~1310, right ~120 px free; no text
  clipped by the frame edge.
- **Sound:** every pop, hop, whoosh, crack and chime in the cue sheet lines up with its visual (spot-check 5 moments).

## Per-scene checklist

1. **One focal point** — within 1 second you know where to look. (Squint test: blur the still — the hero still reads.)
2. **Hierarchy** — clear size contrast between hero text and labels; no more than ~6 display words.
3. **Colour roles respected** — accent only on what matters, < 25 % of the frame; ground differs from the previous scene.
4. **Typography clean** — no awkward breaks, orphans, overflows, clipped descenders, text on busy image areas.
5. **Image quality** — sharp, well-cropped, faces in the upper third, nothing important under captions/UI.
6. **Reveals timed to the voice** — each element appears on its word (check stills at the cue times).
7. **Not front-loaded** — the first still isn't the whole frame; the last still has settled and is readable.
8. **Motif / continuity** — consistent with the video direction; the signature motif present where planned.
9. **Designed, not pasted** — footage in windows with brand bars/ribbons; consistent margins and grid.
10. **Captions band clear** and (9:16) platform safe zones respected.

## Whole-film checks (watch the full sheet in order)

- Rhythm: bursts and at least one breather; no two consecutive scenes with the same layout archetype.
- Colour balance: grounds alternate; the accent doesn't dominate the film.
- The hook frame works with the sound off; the end card holds ≥ 2 s with logo + one clear call to action.
- Facts: every name, number, price and contact matches what the client confirmed.
- Sound (after render): voice clear over music, music bed fades out with the picture, loudness ≈ -14 LUFS.

## Common fixes for weak frames

| Symptom | Redesign |
|---|---|
| Looks empty / template-like | add a texture region, a photo window, or scale the hero word up 1.5× and crop it boldly |
| Too busy | remove one element; move secondary info to a small tracked label |
| Flat, everything equal | create a 3:1 size contrast and a single accent hit |
| Photo feels pasted | put it in a window with a brand bar, add the slow push, crop tighter |
| Same as previous scene | switch archetype (split ↔ full-bleed ↔ centred type ↔ cards) and ground colour |
| Reveal too early/late | move it to the word time in the CUE table |
